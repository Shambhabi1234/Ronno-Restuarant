Ronno — the kitchen
Our Story

Ronno started as a family recipe book that outgrew the kitchen. Every dish on this menu traces back to something cooked at home first — a grandmother's mustard paste, a fish curry that only tastes right in monsoon.

We kept the technique and let the presentation change. Nothing here is fusion for its own sake — it's the same food, given the attention a proper sitting deserves.
